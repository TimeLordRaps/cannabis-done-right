	<h1>Myths &amp; Risks</h1>
	<p class="myths-hero__lede">A sober read on what holds up, what doesn’t, and where caution belongs when a substance starts shaping sleep, attention, and judgment.</p>
	<p class="subtle">This page does not flatten the nuance. It does keep the warning visible.</p>

<section class="section reveal" data-reveal>
	<div class="section-head">
		<p class="eyebrow">Myth reality scan</p>
		<h2>What the hype leaves out</h2>
		<p>These are not moral claims. They are threshold claims: when the effect moves from occasional utility into a pattern that costs you function.</p>
	</div>

	<div class="myths-grid">
		<article class="myths-card">
			<span class="myths-card__badge">Myth 01</span>
			<h3>“Natural means harmless.”</h3>
			<p>Natural is not neutral. Dose, frequency, and context still decide whether a substance supports you or starts steering you.</p>
		</article>

		<article class="myths-card">
			<span class="myths-card__badge">Myth 02</span>
			<h3>“If I’m still functioning, it’s fine.”</h3>
			<p>Function can look intact while sleep, motivation, and decision quality quietly slide. The cost often shows up later than the habit does.</p>
		</article>

		<article class="myths-card">
			<span class="myths-card__badge">Myth 03</span>
			<h3>“Relief now means relief overall.”</h3>
			<p>Short-term calm can mask longer-term amplification of avoidance, dependency, or emotional narrowing.</p>
		</article>
	</div>
</section>

<section class="section reveal" data-reveal>
	<div class="section-head">
		<p class="eyebrow">Risk intelligence</p>
		<h2>The consequential part</h2>
		<p>When a strain or a routine starts to bend judgment, the issue is no longer taste. It is timing, threshold, and consequence.</p>
	</div>

	<div class="grid grid--risks">
		<article class="risk-card">
			<span class="risk-card__label">Dependence</span>
			<h3>Ritual can outrun intention</h3>
			<p>What begins as relief can harden into a pattern that feels necessary precisely because it is repetitive.</p>
			<ul class="risk-card__list">
				<li>Track the trigger, not just the session count.</li>
				<li>Notice whether the use is solving a problem or postponing one.</li>
			</ul>
		</article>

		<article class="risk-card">
			<span class="risk-card__label">Mood stability</span>
			<h3>Activation is a tradeoff, not a gift</h3>
			<p>In vulnerable people, higher activation can sharpen anxiety, paranoia, or volatility faster than it produces clarity.</p>
			<ul class="risk-card__list">
				<li>Lower volatility is usually the safer default.</li>
				<li>Stronger is not the same as better.</li>
			</ul>
		</article>

		<article class="risk-card">
			<span class="risk-card__label">Judgment</span>
			<h3>Memory and attention move first</h3>
			<p>The story you tell yourself can remain persuasive even after your choice quality has already weakened.</p>
			<ul class="risk-card__list">
				<li>Watch sleep, task friction, and follow-through.</li>
				<li>Use evidence instead of vibe to judge the outcome.</li>
			</ul>
		</article>
	</div>
</section>
