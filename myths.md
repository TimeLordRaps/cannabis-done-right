# Cannabis Myths vs Reality (Erowid)

## Key Myths
- **Safe/non-addictive**: Psychological dependence common.
- **No motivation loss**: Amotivational syndrome real in heavy use.
- **No psychosis risk**: Triggers in vulnerable genetics.
