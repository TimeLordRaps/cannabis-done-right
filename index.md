## 🚨 First: Steps to Quit Cannabis Addiction

If your reward circuitry is hacked by habitual all-day use:

1. **Cold Turkey Commitment**: Delete dispensary apps, block sites, tell friends/family for accountability.
2. **Replace the Ritual**: Music/dissociation triggers → journaling, walks, cold showers.
3. **Dopamine Reset**: No screens/social 48hrs, high-protein meals, sunlight/exercise.
4. **Sleep Hygiene**: Melatonin 3mg, no naps, consistent bedtime.
5. **Therapy Anchor**: CBT for dissociation, mindfulness for cravings.
6. **Watch This**: [Scared Straight Quitting Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Brm71uCWr-I)

> "Dissociate to dispensary" is real — your brain wired it as escape. Break the loop.
